Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

SharX does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. The app does not require account registration or login.

When you enter drug or supplement names to check interactions, that input is sent to our API proxy (Cloudflare Worker) solely to process your query and return a result. This data is not logged or retained.

2. Local Storage

SharX stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Your last 5 interaction checks (history)
  • Cached results (up to 7 days, to reduce API calls)
  • Your selected language preference
  • PWA install banner dismissal status

This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser or app storage.

3. Third-Party Services

SharX uses the following third-party services:

  • Anthropic API — processes your drug/supplement query to generate an interaction report. Anthropic's privacy policy applies: anthropic.com/privacy
  • Cloudflare Workers — serves as a proxy between the app and Anthropic API. No user data is logged. Cloudflare's privacy policy: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy
  • Google Analytics — anonymous usage statistics (page views, session counts). No personally identifiable data is collected. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

4. Medical Disclaimer

SharX is intended for informational purposes only and does not constitute medical advice. Always consult a licensed healthcare professional before making any medication decisions.

5. Children's Privacy

SharX is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.
